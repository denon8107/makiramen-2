A story with flavour

YOUR JOURNEY.
OUR BOWL.

Founded by Teddy Lee, Maki & Ramen began with a chef's journey through the craft of sushi in Tokyo. In 2015, that passion became the first Maki & Ramen in Edinburgh.

Today, the brand brings Japanese-inspired ramen and sushi to diners across the UK. The idea remains simple: make delicious, accessible food that brings people together.

One bowl. A whole lot of heart.
